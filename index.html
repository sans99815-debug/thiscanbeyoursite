FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking. Can't find your answer? Call us anytime.

How do you determine the price?
Our pricing is based on how much space your items take up in our truck — not by weight or number of items. Our crew provides a free, no-obligation estimate on-site before any work begins. You approve the price, then we start. No surprises, ever.
Do you offer same-day service?
Yes! We offer same-day service 7 days a week. Book before 12PM and we can usually be there the same afternoon. Call us at (555) 123-4567 for fastest scheduling.
What items do you NOT take?
We cannot accept: hazardous materials (paint, chemicals, asbestos), medical waste, certain electronics (call to confirm), or items with biohazard contamination. For most other items, we can help! Call us if you're unsure about a specific item.
Are you licensed and insured?
Absolutely. JunkAway Pro is fully licensed, bonded, and insured in all areas we operate. We carry general liability and workers' compensation insurance. Proof of insurance is available upon request.
Do I need to be home during pickup?
While we prefer someone to be present for the initial estimate and approval, in some cases we can accommodate contactless service with prior arrangements. Please call us to discuss your specific needs.
What happens to my stuff after you take it?
We sort everything at our facility. Usable items are donated to local charities (Goodwill, Habitat for Humanity, etc.). Recyclable materials go to certified recycling centers. Only what can't be repurposed goes to an EPA-compliant disposal facility.
Do you disassemble furniture?
Yes! Our team is equipped to disassemble furniture, disconnect appliances, and break down large items as needed. This service is included in our labor — no extra charge.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Discover), cash, check, Venmo, Zelle, and PayPal. Payment is collected after the job is complete and you're satisfied.
